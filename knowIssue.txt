FIXED (see tests/test_rules.py and tests/test_perft.py)

  "When the opponent's rat entered my trap, my elephant beside the trap didn't
   dare to attack her. This is a defect to be fixed."

Root cause: engine/rules.py:can_capture rejected Elephant-vs-Rat unconditionally,
in a branch that ran *before* the trap's rank-0 rule was ever consulted. Every
other animal already worked, because they fell through to the rank comparison
where the trapped Rat's effective rank of 0 made the capture legal. Only the
Elephant hit the early return.

It was worse than a missed capture: the mirror call was allowed, so the trapped
Rat could eat the Elephant that could not eat it. The Elephant was hanging.

The rule now reads: the trap zeroes the DEFENDER's rank only. A piece standing in
the enemy's traps can be captured by any adjacent enemy piece, Elephant included,
but it still attacks at its full rank — vulnerable, not disarmed. The Rat-beats-
Elephant exception applies only to a Rat that still holds its real rank.

The board now also shows this: a trapped piece gets a red rank-0 badge and a red
outline, and a capture target that holds a piece gets a green ring drawn on top of
the piece (the old green dot was drawn underneath it, so captures were nearly
invisible).
